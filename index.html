How to

Room: drag a rectangle. Shift+drag cuts rock back in.
Cave: paint cave floor freehand; its walls come out rough and rounded (Shift = erase). Set the brush size under the tools.
Inner wall: drag along the grid lines inside a room. Click a wall piece to remove it, Shift+drag erases.
Corridor: paint floor freehand (Shift = erase).
Doors: click the border between two squares. Click it again to remove the door.
Objects: click a cell. Click stairs again to rotate them.
Right-click a symbol or a door to remove it.
Pan: right-drag, middle-drag or Space+drag.
Zoom: mouse wheel.
The map autosaves in this browser.
